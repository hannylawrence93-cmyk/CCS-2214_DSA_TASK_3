Question (a): (A + (B - C) * D)

Step 1: Identify the operators.
The expression contains addition (+), subtraction (-), and multiplication (*).

Step 2: Convert to postfix.
1. B - C becomes BC-
2. (B - C) * D becomes BC-D*
3. A + (B - C) * D becomes ABC-D*+

Final Postfix Expression:
ABC-D*+

Expression Tree:
          +
         / \
        A   *
           / \
          -   D
         / \
        B   C

The root operator is addition because it is the final operation performed.
