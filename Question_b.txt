Question (b): (A + B) * (C - D)

Step 1: Convert each bracket separately.
1. A + B becomes AB+
2. C - D becomes CD-

Step 2: Combine the expressions using multiplication.
AB+ and CD- are combined using *.

Final Postfix Expression:
AB+CD-*

Expression Tree:
          *
         / \
        +   -
       / \ / \
      A  B C  D
