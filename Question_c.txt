Question (c): (A + B) * (C - D) / E

Step 1: Convert the brackets.
1. A + B becomes AB+
2. C - D becomes CD-

Step 2: Combine using multiplication.
AB+ and CD- become AB+CD-*.

Step 3: Divide the result by E.
Append E and the division operator.

Final Postfix Expression:
AB+CD-*E/
